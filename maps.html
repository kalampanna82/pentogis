Maps we make.

Each map type answers a different question about a place. Pick a type below to see what it shows, who uses it, and what you get.

Slope maps

How steep the ground is, cell by cell, from lidar and elevation data. Shows where land is flat enough to build, where grading gets costly, and where erosion is a risk.

Used for

  • Site selection and buildable area
  • Stormwater and erosion control
  • Road, trail and accessible-route grades

You get: Slope raster, slope-class polygons, print maps, area by class

Land cover and change maps

Classified satellite imagery showing forest, water, farmland, built-up land and more, and how each has changed between dates.

Used for

  • Habitat and open-space change
  • Urban growth and impervious surface
  • Baselines for environmental reports
