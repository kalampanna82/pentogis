About

Where maps, data, and decisions meet.

PentoGIS is a New Jersey-based geospatial technology practice. We combine GIS, remote sensing, AI, statistics, and planning to turn location data into answers people can act on.

The name

Five parts, one picture.

"Pento" means five. Our mark is a pentagon cut into five parcels, because dividing land into meaningful pieces is the basic act of mapping and planning. It also stands for the five disciplines we bring together: GIS, remote sensing, AI, analytics, and planning.
