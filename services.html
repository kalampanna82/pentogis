How we engage

Three ways to work together.

Pick the model that fits the work. We'll recommend one after the first conversation.

Project-based

A defined scope and deliverables, agreed in writing before work starts.

  • Fixed scope and timeline
  • Best for studies, maps, and new tools

Hourly consulting

Flexible time for advice, reviews, and smaller tasks.

  • Pay for the time used
  • Best for reviews, training, and quick fixes

Ongoing retainer

Regular support for data, dashboards, and maps that need upkeep.

  • Reserved monthly capacity
  • Best for recurring reports and maintenance
