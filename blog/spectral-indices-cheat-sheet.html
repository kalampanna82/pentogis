Spectral indices cheat sheet: NDVI, NDWI, NBR and more

The eight remote sensing indices you'll meet most, with formulas and the exact satellite bands for each.

PentoGIS · 24 October 2026 · 5 min read

A spectral index combines two or more bands of a satellite image into one number that highlights something specific: vegetation, water, moisture, buildings or burned land. Most follow the same pattern, a normalized difference (A − B) ÷ (A + B), which keeps values between −1 and 1. This page collects the common ones in one place.

Why different surfaces stand out

Every material reflects light differently across wavelengths, its spectral signature. Indices work by picking two bands where the target behaves very differently from everything else.

  • Vegetation absorbs red and reflects near-infrared (NIR) strongly: the basis of NDVI.
  • Water is dark in NIR and SWIR: the basis of NDWI and MNDWI.
  • Soil and built-up land brighten in shortwave infrared (SWIR): the basis of NDBI.
  • Burned land loses NIR and gains SWIR2: the basis of NBR.

MNDWI(Green − SWIR1) ÷ (Green + SWIR1)Open water, especially near citiesNDWI guide
NDMI(NIR − SWIR1) ÷ (NIR + SWIR1)Moisture in vegetation (also called Gao's NDWI)Gao, 1996
NDBI(SWIR1 − NIR) ÷ (SWIR1 + NIR)Built-up land and bare groundNDBI guide
NBR(NIR − SWIR2) ÷ (NIR + SWIR2)Burned land; dNBR shows severityNBR guide

EVI and SAVI aren't plain normalized differences: their constants (2.5, 6, 7.5, 1 and 0.5) reduce the effects of the atmosphere and of bright soil. Use surface reflectance (atmospherically corrected) data for all of these, not raw digital numbers.

Notice that water scores high on NDMI and NBR too. Both bands are close to zero over water, so the ratio is unstable. That's why water is usually masked out, with NDWI or MNDWI, before using moisture or burn indices.

Tips

  • Compare like with like: same season, same sensor, same processing level.
  • Mask clouds, shadows and water first.
  • Mind resolution: Sentinel-2's SWIR bands are 20 m, so SWIR-based indices are coarser than NDVI at 10 m.
  • Indices are evidence, not answers: check them against known places or field data.

Frequently asked questions

Which index should I start with?

NDVI for vegetation, MNDWI for water, NBR for fire. Most projects use two or three together.

Can I calculate these in free software?

Yes: QGIS's Raster Calculator, Google Earth Engine, or Python with rasterio and NumPy.

Do drones work?

Yes, if the camera records the needed bands. Most multispectral drone cameras include red and NIR, but few have SWIR.
