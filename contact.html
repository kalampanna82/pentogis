FAQ

Common questions.

What should I send with my first message?

Your goal, the area you're interested in, the data you already have, and your timeline. A few lines is enough to start.

Do I need to have my own data?

No. We can work with public sources such as census data, satellite imagery, and open data portals, combine them with your own data, or both.

What will I receive at the end?

Depending on the project: maps (PDF and image files), GIS data (for example shapefile, GeoPackage, or file geodatabase), web maps and dashboards, reports, and code. Everything comes with documentation, and you own it.

Which software do you use?

ArcGIS or open-source tools such as QGIS, Python, and PostGIS. We match what your team already uses where we can.

Do you work with clients outside New Jersey?

Yes. We're based in New Jersey and work with clients remotely.

How is pricing set?

It depends on scope. After a short scoping conversation we propose an approach and an estimate. See how we engage.
